3.1 Hardware Architecture and Safety¶

The hardware controller reads IMU and SPI motor states, runs the state machine and policies, and sends 16-axis commands to two SPI devices. The SDK, WebRTC, and external algorithms communicate with the controller through LCM.

Gamepad / SDK / WebRTC      external algorithm
          |                    |
          v                    v
       Y20W controller <---- DEVELOPMENT LCM
        |    |    |
       SPI  IMU  state LCM
        |
   12 leg joints + 4 wheel joints

Must confirm before running

The emergency stop is effective, the robot is reliably supported, the wheels are off the ground or the area is clear, power is normal, cables cannot be caught by wheels or joints, SPI and IMU device names are correct, and onsite personnel know how to switch to DAMP and cut power immediately.

Hardware safety checks read joint limits from the URDF by default. Do not disable safety_checker.enable_safety_check just to remove errors; locate the model, sensor, or parameter issue first.

Hardware Data Chain¶

  1. Two SPI buses read states from 12 leg joints and 4 wheel joints.
  2. The IMU provides attitude, angular velocity, and acceleration.
  3. The power reader and clock synchronization module provide auxiliary state.
  4. The controller updates the state machine and current policy.
  5. The safety checker validates enabled posture, joint, or leg-state checks.
  6. The controller writes final commands back to SPI and publishes runtime state through LCM.

The Viewer, SDK, WebRTC, and external algorithms all depend on state published by the controller. They do not replace direct checks of SPI, IMU, and the physical emergency stop.

Pre-Power Checklist¶

  • [ ] The robot is fixed by a reliable stand; wheels are off the ground or the motion area is clear.
  • [ ] Emergency stop and power-off paths can be operated immediately by onsite personnel.
  • [ ] Two SPI buses, IMU, CAN/BMS, and clock devices match the configuration.
  • [ ] Power voltage is normal, and cables cannot enter wheel or joint ranges.
  • [ ] The initial mode is DAMP; velocity and attitude commands are zero.
  • [ ] The configuration, model, and URDF have been validated in MuJoCo.
